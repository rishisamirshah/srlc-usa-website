Before you give

Honest answers to the questions donors actually ask.

Yes. SRLC USA is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 81-5162502. Every gift made through srlcusa.org is tax deductible to the extent permitted by law. No goods or services are exchanged in return for your gift.

Within 48 hours of your gift, you receive an IRS-compliant receipt by email. Monthly donors also get a year-end summary in January, listing every gift from the prior calendar year. If a receipt does not arrive, email info@srlcusa.org and we will resend it the same day.

Anytime. There is no minimum commitment, no cancellation fee, and no phone tree. Email info@srlcusa.org or log in to your donor portal to update the amount, change the program, pause, or cancel. Changes take effect on your next billing date.

Every gift goes to the program you designate. If you do not designate, gifts are directed to the Care with the most urgent unmet need that month. Overhead and fundraising costs are benchmarked against the most efficient US nonprofits in our category. See the current ratio on the Financials page.

Yes. Checks payable to SRLC USA can be mailed to 500 Paterson Plank Rd #33685, Union City, NJ 07087. For wire or ACH instructions, or to set up a recurring bank transfer, email info@srlcusa.org and our team will respond within one business day.

Please note that tax-deductibility applies under US law; donors outside the US should consult a local tax adviser about deductibility in their country.
