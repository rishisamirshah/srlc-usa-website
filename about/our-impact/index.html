Independent recognition and accreditation

The movement received Special Consultative Status from the United Nations Economic and Social Council in 2020, and Candid awarded its Gold Seal of Transparency in 2025. Independent recognition matters because it does not depend on our own account of the work. The 250-bed Shrimad Rajchandra Hospital and Research Center earned accreditation from the National Accreditation Board for Hospitals and Healthcare Providers (NABH) within its first year of operation.

SRLC USA is the US chapter of Shrimad Rajchandra Love and Care, which holds Special Consultative Status with the United Nations Economic and Social Council.

An operating model built for efficiency

Because the programs are carried by experienced professionals and volunteers, administration costs remain low, and each initiative is managed against defined performance indicators. In practical terms, this means a greater share of every contribution reaches the programs it was intended to support.

What these figures mean in practice

Laxibhai, a 52-year-old farmer, had been living with triple-vessel heart disease, and the surgery he needed was beyond his family’s means. He received the region’s first cardiothoracic bypass at Shrimad Rajchandra Hospital at no cost, and he has since returned home to his fields. His recovery is one outcome among millions, and it is the kind of outcome every figure on this page represents.
