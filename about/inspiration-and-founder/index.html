Inspiration and Founder

Jainism and Lord Mahavir

Jainism, one of the world’s oldest spiritual philosophies, teaches enduring, universal principles of love and kindness. Its teachings have shaped the lives of millions across the world and remain a living source of inspiration today.

Lord Mahavir, a revered spiritual leader, lived in India around 500 BCE. He illuminated a path to inner peace through understanding and kindness toward all. At the heart of that path is compassion for every living being, the root from which empathy grows. More than 2,500 years later, his message continues to guide the modern world.

Shrimad Rajchandraji

In the late 19th century, the saint and poet-philosopher Shrimad Rajchandraji illuminated the wisdom of Lord Mahavir with remarkable clarity, shaping a spiritual path for a new era. He is described as self-realized: someone who had directly experienced the nature of the soul rather than only studied it.

Mahatma Gandhi called Shrimad Rajchandraji his spiritual guide. Gandhi’s philosophy of truth and non-violence took shape under His influence, and the two exchanged letters on faith and conduct for years. That influence stands as a testament to the enduring impact of His message, which continues to resonate today.

Grounded in direct inner experience, He articulated the subtle truths of spirituality through His own life, valuable teachings, and influential writings. Chief among them is Shri Atmasiddhi Shastra, a verse work on the nature of the soul and the path to self-realization, which blends deep philosophical insight with practical steps for inner transformation.

Pujya Gurudevshri Rakeshji

Legacy of Wisdom

Pujya Gurudevshri Rakeshji is an enlightened visionary, global ambassador of peace, and the founder of Shrimad Rajchandra Mission Dharampur, the spiritual organization from which this movement grew. Following the revered footsteps of Shrimad Rajchandraji, Pujya Gurudevshri carries forward a legacy of timeless wisdom, translating profound spiritual truths into transformative teachings that make spirituality accessible to all.

Steady Force of Compassion

Through Shrimad Rajchandra Love and Care (SRLC), Pujya Gurudevshri translates compassion into action. Guided by His philosophy of empathy and universal harmony, SRLC is a global organization whose initiatives address critical needs in health care, education, environmental sustainability, and social welfare across many countries. In His teaching, inner clarity and outward kindness are one path: what is understood within flows naturally into care for others.

A Movement Reaching America

That vision has taken root across the United States and continues to grow. Through SRLC USA, volunteers in communities nationwide carry this spirit of selfless service into action. They serve their own neighborhoods through the 10 Care Program, and they support institutes in India and initiatives around the world.
