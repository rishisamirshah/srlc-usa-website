About SRLC USA

Management Team

SRLC USA is led by a Board of Trustees who bring experience from medicine, finance, technology, education, and business. Together they set the organization’s direction, oversee its finances, and guide the national campaigns that raise funds and awareness for SRLC’s work in the US, India, and Africa.
