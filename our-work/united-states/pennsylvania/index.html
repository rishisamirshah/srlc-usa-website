Constantly serving communities

Inside the Pennsylvania centers

Food Security & Pantry Support

Near the Shrimad Rajchandra Spiritual Center, volunteers built a reliable rhythm of pantry support: preparing PB&J materials, gathering bakery items (including bagels donated by a local deli), and delivering staples to Christ Episcopal Church Food Pantry, East Stroudsburg Salvation Army, Stroudsburg Wesleyan Pantry & SK, and Swiftwater Eglise Evangelique Renaissance Pantry. Each visit kept shelves a bit fuller and spirits a bit lighter.

Hygiene & Clothing Essentials

Care took practical form with hygiene kits and new clothing assembled and distributed through trusted local channels. Partners included Christ Episcopal Church Food Pantry and Violette Stand for the Children Home, ensuring families received exactly the kind of everyday essentials that ease the month.

Classroom of Change & Youth Support

Volunteers readied kids for learning with school kits, sling bags, and essential kits shared through Monroe County Children Youth and Foster Care and Christ Episcopal Church, supporting students in neighborhoods surrounding the ashram.

Hunger Relief

Our Philadelphia volunteers focus on steady, reliable nourishment to support families facing food insecurity. Through PB&J donations and pantry staple distributions, volunteers partner with organizations such as Somerset County Food Pantry and the People’s Pantry of Malvern to ensure neighbors have access to consistent, nourishing meals.

Education & Community Support

In addition to food relief, volunteers prepare and distribute school bags and essential supplies for students, including deliveries to Dr. Tanner G. Duckrey Public School and Monroe County Children & Youth and Foster Care. The team also supports families through donation drives benefiting the Coatesville Community Youth and Women’s Alliance, helping ensure household essentials remain within reach during challenging times.
