Inside the Illinois centers

Constantly serving communities, one center at a time.

Classroom of Change

At the beginning of each school year, our Chicago team comes together while mobilizing friends and family to fund and assemble hundreds of Classroom of Change kits. Filled with essential supplies like notebooks, pencils, scissors, and gluesticks, these kits are distributed to Title I schools throughout the city, giving local children the tools they need to thrive in the classroom.

Hunger Relief

Our Chicago volunteers also recently joined forces to pack over 500 peanut butter and jelly sandwiches and donate over 1,000 pounds of food. These contributions are delivered to The Salvation Army, Crystal Lake Food Pantry, Veterans Path to Hope, Frida’s Place, and Elgin Cares, ensuring that vulnerable individuals across the city and surrounding communities have access to nourishing meals.
