10 Care Program

Emergency Relief Care

Why It Matters

Emergency Relief Care provides immediate response and long-term rehabilitation to communities affected by natural disasters and crises.

The Work in India

When floods, droughts, or public health emergencies strike, relief arrives in two waves: immediate essentials in the first days, then the long work of rebuilding: schools and homes reconstructed for families who lost everything, dedicated care facilities during the pandemic, and door-to-door support for the communities hardest hit.
