10 Care Program

SRLC’s holistic, multi-pronged community support and development programs are powered by genuine empathy and selfless care by highly motivated volunteers. SRLC constantly endeavors to deliver high quality and sustainable initiatives for the welfare of humans, animals, and the environment.

Health Care

Delivering a charitable hospital and rural medical camps to provide life-changing medical solutions for those lacking access or resources.

Explore Health Care

Educational Care

Equipping underserved children with quality education, unlocking their potential to learn, grow, and lead.

Explore Educational Care

Child Care

Nurturing children’s lives through holistic care that nourishes their well-being, development, and happiness.

Explore Child Care

Woman Care

Transforming women’s lives with vocational training, education, and healthcare that fuel independence and opportunity.

Explore Woman Care

Tribal Care

Supporting Indigenous communities in rural India through far-reaching social and economic programs.

Explore Tribal Care

Community Care

Strengthening local communities through projects such as organ donation campaigns, educational classes, and programs supporting incarcerated individuals.

Explore Community Care

Humanitarian Care

Bringing hope to those in crisis by distributing essential resources, whenever and wherever they are needed most.

Explore Humanitarian Care

Animal Care

Extending compassion and care to injured or abandoned animals through rescue, shelter, and treatment.

Explore Animal Care

Environmental Care

Safeguarding the planet through bold conservation and sustainability efforts, such as tree planting, water conservation, and alternative energy resources.

Explore Environmental Care

Emergency Relief Care

Rapid response with food, medicine, and essentials during floods, earthquakes, pandemics, and more, as well as long-term rehabilitation for affected communities.

Explore Emergency Relief Care
