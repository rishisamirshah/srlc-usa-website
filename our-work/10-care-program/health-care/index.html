Health Care

Health Care delivers comprehensive medical services of high quality, free or deeply subsidized, for those who cannot access or afford them. From a charitable hospital to medical camps deep in rural India, the full spectrum of care reaches the people other systems overlook.

The work in India

Rural communities in South Gujarat rank among the poorest in the country. Health Care covers primary, secondary, and tertiary care for those who cannot access or afford it, anchored by the Shrimad Rajchandra Hospital and Research Center and extended by outreach programs that carry treatment the last mile.

In the United States

SRLC USA chapters bring Health Care home: volunteers assemble hygiene kits for shelters and families in need, deliver comfort items, toys, and books to patients in local hospitals, and run wellness-focused programs for seniors and community members across our chapter cities.


US chapter volunteers · Media Bank

Laxmibhai’s second chance

Laxmibhai, a 52-year-old farmer, was diagnosed with severe triple-vessel heart disease, the kind of diagnosis that ends a working life in a place without care. Instead, he received the region’s first-ever cardiothoracic bypass surgery, performed entirely free of charge. Today he is back on his feet.
