Our Mission

Love and Care. Compassion in action.

Shrimad Rajchandra Love and Care USA is the United States chapter of Shrimad Rajchandra Love and Care, a UN-recognized humanitarian organization. SRLC USA is a 501(c)(3) public charity raising funds across 25+ U.S. cities for global health, education, and humanitarian work. Every gift funds a specific program with a published outcome.

Where the work happens

The institutes you fund

American donations flow into a global network of institutions, each one built around a specific need. Your gift funds their day-to-day work.

Shrimad Rajchandra Hospital and Research Center

A 250-bed NABH-accredited multi-specialty hospital in Dharampur. Most patients receive care for free.

Shrimad Rajchandra Vidyapeeth

The first science college across 238 villages, opening higher education to tribal youth.

Shrimad Rajchandra Gurukul

A secondary and higher secondary rural school offering holistic education.

Shrimad Rajchandra Skill Development Center

Free vocational and soft-skills training for tribal youth, leading to employment.

Shrimad Rajchandra Jivamaitridham

An upcoming animal sanctuary with a 150-ward animal hospital, veterinary college, and animal shelters.

Shrimad Rajchandra Center of Excellence for Women

An upcoming, state-of-the-art facility dedicated to the holistic development and empowerment of tribal women.
